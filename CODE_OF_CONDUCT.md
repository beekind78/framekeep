# Code of conduct

This policy applies to Framekeep's issues, pull requests, and other project discussions.

- Treat contributors with respect. Welcome beginner questions and differing opinions.
- Keep feedback about the work. Explain disagreements without personal attacks.
- Respect others' privacy. Share someone's personal information only with their permission.
- Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, and sexual comments directed at others are unacceptable.

If you need help with a conduct concern, ask the maintainer for a private contact through a GitHub issue and omit names and details from the public request. For abusive content on GitHub, you can also use [GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

The maintainer may remove harmful content, close a discussion, or block accounts to protect participants.
